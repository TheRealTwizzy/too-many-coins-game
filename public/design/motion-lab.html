Motion spec

Timing tokens

  • 150ms micro (hover, press)
  • 300ms movement (rows, panels)
  • 600ms moments (drops, ignition)
  • 5s the tick heartbeat — the page breathes at game cadence

Curves

  • Gains overshoot: cubic-bezier(.2,1.4,.4,1)
  • Losses never bounce: ease-in, then gone
  • Ambient loops are linear and slow

Principles

  • Every animation is a mechanic made visible — no decoration without meaning
  • Drama is information, never magnitude: the ticker names acts, not amounts
  • Red pulses belong to the blackout alone
  • All motion collapses under prefers-reduced-motion
